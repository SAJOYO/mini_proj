# English Pointer Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (liver and white)

```
  Base coat        #EDE3D6
  Liver patches    #6E4630
  Ticking / speckle#A98366
  Nose / eyes /
  mouth            #33261E
  Outline          #6B5343
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- LIVER AND WHITE: a white base coat broken by a few large solid LIVER patches - one over an eye and ear, one or two across the back - with fine liver TICKING speckled over the white in between
- Long soft DROP ears set level with the eyes, hanging flat against the cheeks with slightly pointed tips
- A long deep muzzle with a pronounced stop and open nostrils, on a head carried high
- A lean athletic build, longer than tall, with a level topline and a straight tapering tail carried straight out LEVEL with the back

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | rolled belly up |
| Adolescent | front paws up on the knee |
| Young adult | walking side by side |
| Senior | floor sprawl |

either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual

## Notes

- Ticking is what separates a Pointer from a plain patched dog. If the white comes out clean, repeat "fine liver ticking speckled across the white" at the end of the prompt.
- Keep the patches few and large. Many small patches turn it into a Springer or a Dalmatian.
- Do not curl the tail. A Pointer's tail is carried straight out level with the back - lifting it into an arc reads as a hound.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
