# Cavalier King Charles Spaniel Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (ruby)

```
  Body coat        #B5713F
  Feathering /
  chest            #DCA772
  Ear shading      #96562C
  Nose / eyes /
  mouth            #241A14
  Outline          #6A422A
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Solid RUBY colour - a warm rich chestnut over the whole body with no white markings at all (white would make it a Blenheim, a different variety)
- Very large, round, dark, wide-set eyes on a gently rounded head with a SHALLOW stop and a short tapered muzzle
- Long DROP ears set high, hung with abundant silky FEATHERING that spills well past the jaw and frames the whole face
- A small square toy build with silky feathering on the legs, the feet and the tail, and a plumed tail carried level in constant motion

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | drowsing in the lap |
| Adolescent | rolled belly up |
| Young adult | front paws up on the knee |
| Senior | chin on the arm |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text

A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing
wide enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time.
The dog is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are
clearly visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind.
Only the far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Cavalier King Charles Spaniel puppy
Curled up drowsing in the person's lap, body folded into a tight
comma, head tucked against their forearm, one paw hanging loose over
their knee. The person sits upright, NOT hunched over the dog, and
only tips their chin down a little so their face stays open to the
camera. The dog's eyes are heavy-lidded and half open, drifting up
toward them. Sleepy, heavy, unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a very short tapered
muzzle and a soft baby-round skull. The DROP ears are already
oversized, set high and hanging against the cheeks, covered in short
soft fluff with no feathering yet. Short silky puppy coat lying flat.
Plump round belly, no waist, short stubby legs and oversized paws.
Enormous round wide-set dark eyes #241A14 with a white highlight dot
upper-right, dominating the face, a small dark nose, tiny closed
mouth. Solid RUBY chestnut everywhere with no white, and a short tail
with a small tuft beginning at the tip. Innocent, drowsy,
uncoordinated.
Coat pale ruby #C68A5A; chest and muzzle #DCA772; ears deeper chestnut
#96562C; nose, eyes, mouth #241A14; one uniform thick warm brown
outline #6A422A.
```

## Notes

- The huge round eyes and the short tapered muzzle are what make this read as a Cavalier rather than a small spaniel. Keep the stop shallow - a deep stop turns it into a King Charles.
- Do not add white. A white blaze or chest patch changes the variety and breaks the palette.
- Feathering is silky and straight, never curly. Curls turn it into a Poodle cross.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
