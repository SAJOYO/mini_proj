# Border Collie Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (black and white)

```
  Body coat        #3A3733
  White markings   #F5F1E8
  Mid shading      #565149
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #241F1C
  Outline          #4A443D
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- The IRISH MARKING PATTERN: black over the head, back and sides, with a white blaze running up the muzzle between the eyes, a wide white collar around the neck, a white chest, white paws and a white tail tip
- SEMI-ERECT ears: standing up at the base and folding over at the top third - neither fully pricked nor fully dropped
- Moderate feathering on the tail, chest and the back of the legs, with a smooth close face - NOT a full silky coat
- A low, intense, focused stare with the head carried level

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | play bow, about to spring |
| Adolescent | front paws up on the knee |
| Young adult | sitting side by side |
| Senior | leaning its weight against the leg |

## Notes

- The Irish marking pattern is what makes this read as a Border Collie at a glance. If the white goes patchy or random, repeat "white blaze up the muzzle, wide white collar, white chest, white paws, white tail tip" at the end of the prompt.
- Keep the feathering MODERATE and the face smooth. Too much and it becomes a Rough Collie or a Sheltie; too little and it becomes a generic black-and-white farm dog.
- The famous "eye" (the low, hard herding stare) is fine for the young adult, but do not push it in the other stages - it fights the warm family mood.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
