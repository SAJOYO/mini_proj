# Korean Jindo Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (white Jindo)

```
  Body coat        #F4EFE5
  Shading / undercoat #DFD4C2
  Ears / back tone #E7DECD
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #2A2521
  Outline          #6E6152
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Medium-large, SQUARE, athletic build - clearly bigger and longer-legged than a Shiba Inu, with a deep chest and a level back
- ERECT triangular ears set wide on a broad forehead, pointing slightly forward
- A thick tail carried in a firm SICKLE curve up over the back (a raised arc, not a flat coiled ring)
- Clean short double coat with NO long feathering; almond eyes set slightly slanted, calm and watchful

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | play bow, about to spring |
| Adolescent | walking side by side |
| Young adult | sitting side by side |
| Senior | leaning its weight against the leg |

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing wide
enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time. The dog
is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are clearly
visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind. Only the
far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Korean Jindo puppy
Front end dropped low to the ground, chest and elbows down, rump high
in the air, mid-bounce and about to spring. Three-quarter view, body
angled away from the camera. The person crouches opposite, hands out,
mid-laugh, their face in frame and turned toward the dog. The dog's
head is up and cocked to one side, locked on them. Springy, coiled,
unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle, far
rounder and softer than the adult wedge. ERECT triangular ears still
soft and only half standing, tipping at the points. Thick plush puppy
double coat standing out evenly, no feathering. Plump round belly, no
waist, sturdy short legs and oversized paws. Huge round dark eyes
#2A2521 with a white highlight dot upper-right, small dark oval nose,
tiny closed mouth. Short thick tail beginning to lift into a curve.
Innocent, bold, uncoordinated.
Coat white #F4EFE5; shading and undercoat #DFD4C2; ears #E7DECD; nose,
eyes, mouth #2A2521; one uniform thick warm grey outline #6E6152.
```

## Notes

- The Jindo is easily collapsed into a Shiba Inu by the model. The separators are SIZE and TAIL: the Jindo is medium-large, square and longer-legged with a raised SICKLE curve tail; the Shiba is small, compact, with a tight coiled RING tail. If the output looks small and stubby, repeat "medium-large athletic build, long clean legs, sickle-curved tail" at the end.
- This set is the WHITE Jindo. For the fawn/red variety swap the coat to #D9AE79 with cream #F0E2CB underside, in all four stages.
- The senior's frosting must stay a soft grey shadow, not a yellow stain - the coat is already white.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
