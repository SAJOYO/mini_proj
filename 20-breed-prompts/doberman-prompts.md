# Doberman Pinscher Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (black and rust)

```
  Body coat        #2B2422
  Tan points
  (muzzle / brows /
  chest / paws)    #8E5230
  Highlight        #B87A4E
  Nose / eyes /
  mouth            #17120F
  Outline          #4A3A32
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- BLACK AND RUST: a near-black body with sharply bounded RUST points on the muzzle, the eyebrows, the throat, the chest and the feet - marked places, never random spots or a patchy coat
- Tall NARROW ERECT ears standing straight up and tapering to fine points, unusually long for the head
- A long narrow wedge muzzle on a lean dry head, with a level topline and a deep chest over a hard tucked waist
- A very short DOCKED tail carried high, and a short hard glossy coat with no feathering anywhere

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | front paws up on the knee |
| Adolescent | play bow, about to spring |
| Young adult | walking side by side |
| Senior | chin on the arm |

```text

A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing
wide enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time.
The dog is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are
clearly visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind.
Only the far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Doberman Pinscher puppy
Standing on its hind legs with both front paws planted on the person's
knee, stretching upward, body fully extended and slightly off-balance.
The person bends toward it, both hands reaching in, their face in
frame and laughing down at it. The dog's head is tilted up and back,
gaze locked on their face. Eager, pushy, unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle, far
rounder and softer than the adult wedge. The ears are still soft and
FOLDED over, not yet erect, sitting high on the head. Short hard
glossy coat lying flat everywhere. Plump round belly, no waist, short
thick legs and oversized paws. Huge round dark eyes #17120F with a
white highlight dot upper-right, a small dark nose, tiny closed mouth.
The BLACK AND RUST pattern is already set - rust on the muzzle, the
eyebrows, the throat, the chest and the feet, black everywhere else -
and the short docked tail wags high. Innocent, bold, uncoordinated.
Coat near-black #2B2422; muzzle, brows, throat, chest, paws rust
#8E5230; rust highlight #B87A4E; nose, eyes, mouth #17120F; one
uniform thick warm charcoal outline #4A3A32.
```

## Notes

- The rust points are the whole read. If they blur into the black or scatter across the body, repeat "sharply bounded rust markings on the muzzle, eyebrows, throat, chest and feet only" at the end of the prompt.
- Keep the build lean. Widening the chest turns it into a Rottweiler.
- Puppy ears are NOT yet erect - they are still soft and folded. Standing ears on the puppy stage make it read as an adult shrunk down.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
