# Mixed-Breed Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (warm ochre)

```
  Body coat        #C9A227
  Muzzle / chest /
  paws             #E8CE83
  Ears / shading   #A8871F
  Nose / eyes /
  mouth            #2E2820
  Outline          #6B5622
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Deliberately GENERIC - a friendly mixed-breed dog that must not read as any one recognisable breed
- SEMI-DROP ears set at about forty-five degrees: not standing erect and not hanging flat, the tips folding softly outward
- A medium muzzle of ordinary length, a medium build of ordinary proportion - nothing exaggerated in either direction
- A plain SOLID warm ochre coat, short and smooth, with no markings, no saddle and no patches, and a medium tail carried in a relaxed easy curve

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | chin on the arm |
| Adolescent | play bow, about to spring |
| Young adult | walking side by side |
| Senior | leaning in |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing
wide enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time.
The dog is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are
clearly visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind.
Only the far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a mixed-breed puppy
Sitting close beside the person with its chin dropped onto their
forearm, the full weight of the head resting on them. Near-profile
view. The person's other hand comes over to cradle the side of its
face, their own face in frame and tipped toward the dog. The dog's
eyes are half-closed, drifting up toward them. Heavy, affectionate,
unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle.
SEMI-DROP ears already at their halfway angle, soft and loose, the
tips folding outward. Short smooth puppy coat lying flat everywhere,
no markings anywhere. Plump round belly, no waist, short thick legs
and oversized paws. Huge round dark eyes #2E2820 with a white
highlight dot upper-right, a small dark oval nose, tiny closed mouth.
A pale muzzle, chest and paws against the plain ochre coat, and a
short tail curling in a loose relaxed arc. Innocent, ordinary,
uncoordinated.
Coat warm ochre #DDBB5C; muzzle, chest, paws pale cream #E8CE83; ears
and shading #A8871F; nose, eyes, mouth #2E2820; one uniform thick warm
brown outline #6B5622.
```

## Notes

- This is the fallback used before a breed is chosen, so it must stay unremarkable. If it starts reading as a Shiba or a Retriever, push the ear angle back toward halfway and flatten the tail curl.
- Do not add markings. The moment a saddle or a patch appears it becomes a specific breed and stops working as the neutral origin.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
