# Labrador Retriever Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (yellow Labrador)

```
  Body coat        #E0BE86
  Chest / muzzle   #F3E4C8
  Ears / shading   #C6A067
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #3A2E24
  Outline          #715734
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- SHORT, DENSE, FLAT double coat with NO feathering anywhere - this is the main separator from a Golden Retriever
- A thick OTTER TAIL: round in section, very thick at the base, tapering to the tip, carried straight and level. Never plumed, never fringed.
- A broad clean skull with a pronounced stop and a wide powerful muzzle
- Drop ears set well back and fairly high, hanging close to the head and noticeably SMALL relative to the head

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | carried against the chest |
| Adolescent | front paws up on the knee |
| Young adult | walking side by side |
| Senior | chin resting on the person's arm |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing wide
enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time. The dog
is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are clearly
visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind. Only the
far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Labrador Retriever puppy
Held up against the person's chest, scooped under the front legs, the
body hanging soft and relaxed against them. The person's face, arms and
upper body are all in frame, their cheek close to the puppy's head. The
puppy's head rests back against their shoulder, its face right beside
theirs, giving the camera a soft sleepy glance. Limp, content, unposed.
Oversized broad round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle
and a soft loose jowl. Small floppy drop ears set well back, hanging
close to the head. Short dense plush puppy coat lying flat everywhere,
no feathering at all. Plump round barrel belly, no waist, thick short
legs and comically oversized paws. Huge round warm brown eyes #3A2E24
with a white highlight dot upper-right, a broad dark oval nose, tiny
closed mouth. Cream chest patch, a short thick tail already round and
solid at the base. Innocent, chunky, uncoordinated.
Coat pale yellow #EFD3A4; muzzle, chest, paws cream #F3E4C8; ears and
shading #C6A067; nose, eyes, mouth #3A2E24; one uniform thick warm
brown outline #715734.
```

## Notes

- The model will constantly try to turn this into a Golden Retriever. The two hard separators are the COAT (short, flat, no feathering anywhere) and the TAIL (thick round otter tail, never plumed). If feathering appears, repeat both at the very end of the prompt.
- This set is the YELLOW Labrador. For black swap to #3B3936 body with #55524D shading; for chocolate use #6B4630 with #8A5C40. Change all four stages together.
- A full-grown Labrador is far too large to be picked up. Keep the "carried" pose on the puppy stage only.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
