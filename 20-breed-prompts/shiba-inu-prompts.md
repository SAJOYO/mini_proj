# Shiba Inu Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (red sesame)

```
  Body coat        #C97F45
  Urajiro cream    #F1E3CE
  Ears / back tone #A66233
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #2B221D
  Outline          #6A452A
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Small ERECT triangular ears, thick and tilted slightly FORWARD
- A thick plush tail curled up in a firm RING over the back
- URAJIRO: cream-white markings on the cheeks, the sides of the muzzle, the throat, the chest and the whole underside, with a clean boundary against the red
- Compact square body, straight strong legs, thick short double coat with no feathering; small deep-set triangular eyes with a confident, slightly smug expression

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | floor sprawl |
| Adolescent | sitting side by side |
| Young adult | leaning its weight against the leg |
| Senior | walking side by side |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing wide
enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time. The dog
is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are clearly
visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind. Only the
far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Shiba Inu puppy
Lying belly-down and completely melted into the floor, hind legs kicked
straight out behind in a loose frog-leg splay, front legs stretched
forward. The chin rests directly on the ground between the front paws.
The person sits right beside the dog, one hand resting on its back,
their face turned down toward it and fully in frame. The dog's head is
laid slightly off-center, eyes looking softly up toward them. Boneless,
unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a very short blunt muzzle -
much rounder and softer than the adult fox face. Small ERECT triangular
ears, thick and rounded at the tips, only just standing upright. Thick
soft puppy double coat standing out all over, no feathering. Plump
round belly, no waist, short thick legs and oversized paws. Huge round
dark eyes #2B221D with a white highlight dot upper-right, small dark
oval nose, tiny closed mouth. URAJIRO cream on the cheeks, muzzle,
throat, chest and underside. Short thick tail beginning to curl over
the back. Innocent, sturdy, uncoordinated.
Coat red #C97F45; urajiro cheeks, muzzle, chest, underside cream
#F1E3CE; ears and back deeper #A66233; nose, eyes, mouth #2B221D; one
uniform thick warm brown outline #6A452A.
```

## Notes

- The two features that separate a Shiba from a generic fox-type dog are the URAJIRO cream markings and the tight RING tail. If either goes missing, repeat them at the end of the prompt.
- Do not let the tail read as a plume or a brush. It is thick, plush and curled into a ring lying against the back.
- A Jindo looks similar but is noticeably larger and longer-legged with a sickle tail - keep the Shiba compact and square.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
