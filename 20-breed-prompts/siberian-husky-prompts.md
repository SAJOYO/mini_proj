# Siberian Husky Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (black and white)

```
  Body coat        #4A4844
  White markings   #F2EDE4
  Mid shading      #6E6B65
  Eyes (ice blue)  #8FBFD6
  Nose/mouth       #221F1C
  Outline          #45413B
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- The facial MASK: a dark cap over the skull and around the eyes with crisp white "goggle" markings above the eyes and a white blaze down the muzzle - a hard-edged, symmetrical pattern
- Pale ICE-BLUE almond eyes, set slightly slanted and obliquely
- ERECT triangular ears set high and close together, thick and well-furred with slightly rounded tips
- A thick plush double coat and a full BRUSH TAIL carried in a relaxed sickle curve, never coiled flat against the back

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | play bow, about to spring |
| Adolescent | walking side by side |
| Young adult | sitting side by side |
| Senior | leaning its weight against the leg |

## Notes

- The mask and the blue eyes carry the breed. If the face comes out plain, repeat "sharp-edged facial mask - dark cap, crisp white goggles above the eyes, white blaze down the muzzle" at the end of the prompt.
- Keep the tail a loose SICKLE curve. A tail coiled flat against the back turns it into an Akita or a Shiba.
- Do not let the build get heavy. The Husky is lean and light-boned; a thick heavy body reads as an Alaskan Malamute.
- Brown eyes are also correct for the breed, but keep the ice-blue consistent across all four stages or the set stops reading as one dog.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
