# Miniature Schnauzer Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (salt and pepper)

```
  Body coat        #8C8880
  Beard / legs     #DAD5CA
  Eyebrows / mid   #A9A399
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #262320
  Outline          #4E4A44
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Dramatic bushy EYEBROWS hanging forward over the eyes and a long full BEARD on a rectangular head - the face is the whole breed
- A long RECTANGULAR head with a flat skull and a blunt-ended muzzle, not tapered and not domed
- Harsh WIRY salt-and-pepper coat lying close on the back and sides, with soft pale silver FURNISHINGS on the legs, beard and underbody
- Small V-shaped ears folding forward against the skull, and a compact square terrier body

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | drowsing in the person's lap |
| Adolescent | play bow, about to spring |
| Young adult | leaning its weight against the leg |
| Senior | chin resting on the person's arm |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing wide
enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time. The dog
is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are clearly
visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind. Only the
far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Miniature Schnauzer puppy
Curled up drowsing in the person's lap, body folded into a tight comma,
head tucked against their forearm, one paw hanging loose over their
knee. The person sits upright, NOT hunched over the dog, and only tips
their chin down a little so their face stays open to the camera. The
dog's eyes are heavy-lidded and half open, drifting up toward them.
Sleepy, heavy, unposed.

COMPOSITION: vertical portrait crop. The person's head sits near the
top of the frame with clear space above it, and the dog curled in their
lap sits near the bottom. The frame runs from above the crown of the
person's head down past their lap, so BOTH the whole face and the whole
dog are inside it at once. Never show the person as a headless or
faceless torso, and never crop the top of their head.
Oversized head (1.3x body width), already faintly rectangular but still
soft and round at the corners. The EYEBROWS and BEARD are only just
sprouting - short soft fuzz over the brow and around the muzzle rather
than the full furnishings. Small V ears folded forward, oversized for
the head. Soft dark puppy coat, not yet wiry, standing out evenly.
Plump round belly, short thick legs with soft pale fuzz. Huge round
dark eyes #262320 with a white highlight dot upper-right, half hidden
under the brow fuzz, small dark oval nose, tiny closed mouth. Innocent,
scruffy, uncoordinated.
Body salt-and-pepper #8C8880; beard, brows, legs pale silver #DAD5CA;
mid shading #A9A399; nose, eyes, mouth #262320; one uniform thick dark
grey outline #4E4A44.
```

## Notes

- Eyebrows and beard are the whole breed. If the face comes out clean or generically shaggy, repeat "dramatic bushy eyebrows hanging over the eyes and a long full beard on a rectangular head" at the end of the prompt.
- Keep the head RECTANGULAR with a blunt muzzle end. A tapered muzzle turns it into a Scottish Terrier or a Cairn.
- This set is SALT AND PEPPER. For black swap to #38352F body with #4E4A44 furnishings; for black-and-silver keep the black body but push the beard and legs to #E8E5DE. Change all four stages together.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
