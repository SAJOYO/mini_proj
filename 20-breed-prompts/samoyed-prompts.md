# Samoyed Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (pure white)

```
  Body coat        #F8F5EF
  Shading / undercoat #E2DBCE
  Warm biscuit tips#EFE6D4
  Nose/eyes/mouth  #2B2724
  Outline          #7E786D
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- The SAMOYED SMILE: the corners of the mouth curl permanently upward into a soft grin
- A thick standing double coat, dense and profuse, standing straight OUT from the body all over - not flat, not curly, not scalloped
- ERECT triangular ears, thick and ROUNDED at the tips, set wide
- A heavy plumed tail carried curled up and over the back, and coal black nose and eyes against the pure white coat

## Poses Used Here

| Stage | Pose |
|---|---|
| Puppy | rolled over belly up |
| Adolescent | walking side by side |
| Young adult | sitting side by side |
| Senior | floor sprawl |

## 1. Puppy / 유아기

```text
35mm film-look vector composited in.
A person on a sunlit wooden floor by a window, close beside the dog.
Sheer curtain light, dust motes, warm morning sunlight, blurred potted
plant far in the background, fine grain.

Both the person and the dog are fully inside the frame together, both
in sharp focus on the same focal plane, physically touching or within
arm's reach - this is a photo of the two of them, not a dog photo with
someone in the background. THE PERSON'S FACE MUST BE FULLY VISIBLE:
their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing wide
enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time. The dog
is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are clearly
visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind. Only the
far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Samoyed puppy
Rolled over onto its back, all four legs folded up in the air, spine
twisted, head upside-down and tipped back. The person crouches over it,
one hand on its belly, face in frame and laughing down at it. Mouth
open in an upside-down grin, eyes rolled back toward the camera in a
happy upside-down glance. Ridiculous, undignified, unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle. Small
ERECT triangular ears, thick and very rounded at the tips, only just
standing upright and lost in the surrounding fluff. Dense soft puppy
double coat standing out in every direction, so the whole dog reads as
a white cloud with legs. Plump round belly, no waist, thick short legs
and oversized paws. Huge round coal-black eyes #2B2724 with a white
highlight dot upper-right, a small coal-black nose, and the corners of
the tiny mouth already curling up into the SAMOYED SMILE. Short thick
tail beginning to curl over the back. Innocent, beaming, uncoordinated.
Coat pure white #F8F5EF; undercoat shading #E2DBCE; warm biscuit tips
#EFE6D4; nose, eyes, mouth #2B2724; one uniform thick warm grey outline
#7E786D.
```

## Notes

- The smile and the standing coat are the breed. If the mouth comes out neutral, repeat "the corners of the mouth curled upward into the Samoyed smile" at the end of the prompt.
- The coat must stand OUT from the body. Any wording that makes it lie flat collapses the silhouette toward a white Jindo or a Husky.
- Against a pure white coat the senior signal has to be a soft ivory shadow, never a yellow stain - keep it faint.
- A full-grown Samoyed is far too large to be picked up or held in a lap. Both of those poses are excluded from this set.
- Do not put a dog-eye-level camera instruction back in - it fights the face requirement and crops the person's head.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
