HOW TO USE
  Each breed prompt has two swappable slots:

     [[FRAMING]]   <- always the same block, see below
     [[POSE]]      <- pick ONE pose at random from the library below

  Pick the pose in your own code and string-substitute it into the
  prompt. Do NOT paste the whole list into the prompt and ask the model
  to "choose one" - image models blend the options instead of picking,
  and you get a dog with six legs.

  Every pose below is breed-neutral and drops in as-is. Check the
  STAGE column before picking: not every pose suits every age.

====================================================================
[[FRAMING]]  - paste into every prompt, never changes
====================================================================

FRAMING - BOTH SUBJECTS TOGETHER: this is a photo of the two of them,
not a dog photo with someone in the background. The person and the dog
are both fully inside the frame, both in sharp focus on the same focal
plane, physically touching or within arm's reach. Medium shot taken at
roughly the dog's eye level. The dog reads as the main subject, but the
person's body and hands are clearly visible and actively part of the
picture - never reduced to a blurred shape behind. Only the far
background falls out of focus.

STAGE KEY:  P = puppy   A = adolescent   Y = young adult   S = senior

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 01 - FLOOR SPRAWL          STAGES: P A Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Lying belly-down and completely melted into the floor, hind legs kicked
straight out behind in a loose frog-leg splay, front legs stretched
forward. The chin rests directly on the floor between the front paws.
The person sits on the floor right beside the dog, one hand resting on
its back. Head laid slightly off-center, not squared to the camera,
eyes looking softly up from floor level. Boneless, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 02 - LEANING IN            STAGES: P A Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Sitting upright pressed against the person's leg, the whole body
leaning into them for balance, head tipped all the way back to look up
at their face. Three-quarter view, body angled away from the camera.
The person's hand rests on the dog's chest or shoulder. Looking at
THEM, never at the camera. Trusting, settled, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 03 - PAWS UP               STAGES: P A Y
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Standing on its hind legs with both front paws planted on the person's
knee, stretching upward, body fully extended and slightly off-balance.
The person leans down toward it, both hands reaching in. Head tilted
up and back, gaze locked on their face, never on the camera. Eager,
pushy, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 04 - WALKING TOGETHER      STAGES: A Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Mid-stride walking alongside the person, near-profile view, one front
paw lifted mid-step, body clearly moving through the frame. The person
walks beside it in step, one hand hanging down near its head. The dog's
head is turned up and sideways toward them. Gaze on THEM or off-frame,
never on the camera. Loose, in motion, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 05 - ASLEEP IN THE LAP     STAGES: P Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Curled up asleep in the person's lap, body folded into a tight comma,
head tucked against their forearm, one paw hanging loose over their
knee. The person's hands rest on the dog, their torso and folded legs
clearly in frame. Eyes fully closed. Deeply asleep, heavy, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 06 - CARRIED               STAGES: P A S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Held up against the person's chest, scooped under the front legs, the
body hanging soft and relaxed against them. The person's arms and upper
body fill one side of the frame. The dog's head rests back against
their shoulder, gaze drifting off-frame to the side, never on the
camera. Limp, content, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 07 - BELLY UP              STAGES: P A Y
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Rolled over onto its back on the floor, all four legs folded up in the
air, spine twisted, head upside-down and tipped back. The person
crouches over it, one hand on its belly. Mouth open in an upside-down
grin, eyes squinted shut or looking off-frame, never at the camera.
Ridiculous, undignified, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 08 - SIDE BY SIDE          STAGES: A Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Sitting shoulder to shoulder with the person, both seen from behind at
a three-quarter angle, both looking away from the camera out at the
same distant view. The dog's head is in profile, ears and coat catching
the light. The person's arm rests along the dog's back. Neither faces
the camera. Quiet, companionable, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 09 - PLAY BOW              STAGES: P A Y
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Front end dropped low to the ground, chest and elbows down, rump and
tail high in the air, mid-bounce and about to spring. Three-quarter
view, body angled away from the camera. The person crouches opposite,
hands out, mid-laugh. The dog's head is up and cocked to one side,
locked on THEM, never on the camera. Springy, coiled, unposed.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
POSE 10 - CHIN ON THE ARM       STAGES: P Y S
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Sitting or lying close beside the person with its chin dropped onto
their forearm or thigh, the full weight of the head resting on them.
Near-profile view. The person's other hand comes over to cradle the
side of its face. Eyes half-closed, gaze drifting off-frame, never on
the camera. Heavy, affectionate, unposed.

NOTES
- Every pose already carries the anti-stare rule ("never at the
  camera"). Keep that clause when editing - dropping it brings back
  the flat front-facing avatar look.
- Poses 03, 07 and 09 are high-motion. If the coat texture gets
  smeared, add "frozen mid-motion, sharp, no motion blur on the dog".
- Pose 06 (carried) hides the legs and tail. Skip it if the shot needs
  to show the full body silhouette of the breed.
- Pose 08 shows the dog from behind. It is the only pose where the
  face is not readable - do not use it as the single hero image of a
  life stage.
