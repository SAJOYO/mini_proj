# Greyhound Mascot — 4 Life Stage Prompts

> Ready to send as-is. Pose, framing and gaze are already written in.
> Human in the scene is gender-neutral ("a person", they/them).
> Their clothing, hairstyle and appearance are deliberately unspecified -
> only their pose, their face and their contact with the dog are
> described. The person's face is required to be fully in frame.

## Palette (fawn)

```
  Body coat        #C3AE93
  Chest / muzzle /
  toes             #E4D8C4
  Shading          #9C8666
  Nose / eyes /
  mouth            #2C2622
  Outline          #6E5C48
```

## Breed Anchors (never swap in other breeds' wording)

- Small ROSE EARS folded back flat against the neck, only lifting halfway when alert - never standing erect and never hanging like a spaniel's
- A very long narrow muzzle on a flat narrow skull, with large dark eyes set obliquely
- An extreme sighthound outline: a very deep chest dropping past the elbow, a dramatic ARCHED loin and a severely TUCKED waist, on long fine legs
- A very long thin tail carried LOW in a slight upward curve at the tip, and a short fine coat close enough to show the ribs and the muscle beneath

|---|---|
| Puppy | carried against the chest |
| Adolescent | walking side by side |
| Young adult | rolled belly up |
| Senior | drowsing in the lap |

their whole head, chin to crown, sits inside the frame, never cropped
at the neck, at the shoulders or by the top edge. Pull the framing
wide enough to hold the person's head and the dog at the same time.
The dog is the main subject, but the person's face, body and hands are
clearly visible and part of the picture, never a blurred shape behind.
Only the far background falls out of focus.

GAZE - unforced and natural. The person is either looking down at the
dog with a soft expression or glancing toward the camera. The dog is
either looking up at the person or giving the camera a relaxed, candid
glance with its head tilted off-axis. The two eyelines must make sense
together. If either one looks at the camera it reads as a casual
snapshot glance, never a stiff, symmetrical, front-facing portrait
stare.

THE DOG: a Greyhound puppy
Held up against the person's chest, scooped under the front legs, the
body hanging soft and relaxed against them. The person's face, arms
and upper body are all in frame, their cheek close to the dog's head.
The dog's head rests back against their shoulder, its face right
beside theirs, giving the camera a soft sleepy glance. Limp, content,
unposed.
Oversized round head (1.3x body width) with a short blunt muzzle, far
rounder than the adult's narrow wedge. Small ROSE EARS already folded
back against the neck, soft and loose. Short fine puppy coat lying
flat. Plump round belly with only the faintest hint of a waist, short
legs that already look a size too long, and oversized paws. Huge round
dark eyes #2C2622 with a white highlight dot upper-right, a small dark
nose, tiny closed mouth. Plain fawn throughout with a pale muzzle,
chest and toes, and a long thin tail hanging low. Innocent, gangly,
uncoordinated.
Coat pale fawn #D6C3A8; muzzle, chest, toes cream #E4D8C4; shading
#9C8666; nose, eyes, mouth #2C2622; one uniform thick warm brown
outline #6E5C48.

## Notes

- The tuck and the arched loin are the silhouette. If the body comes out level and barrel-sided, repeat "very deep chest, dramatically tucked waist, arched loin" at the end of the prompt.
- Do not stand the ears up. Erect ears turn it into a Pharaoh Hound or an Ibizan.
- The coat is thin enough that the frame shows through. Keep a hint of rib and hip - smoothing it into a solid tube loses the breed.
- To reshuffle the poses, swap only the paragraph that starts right after "THE DOG:". Ten alternates are in pose-library.txt.
